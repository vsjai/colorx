Delete Your ColorX Data & Progress

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

At ColorX, we prioritize the privacy and safety of children and families. ColorX does not require account registration or profile creation. However, if you or your child wish to clear all game data, saved coloring artwork, or cached diagnostics, you can do so easily using the instructions below.

Method 1: Instant In-App / Device Deletion (Recommended)

Because drawings and coloring records are stored directly on your phone or tablet, you can delete everything immediately without waiting:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps (or Apps & Notifications) and select ColorX.
  3. Select Storage & Cache.
  4. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data). This permanently erases all saved drawings, custom palettes, and local game progress.
  5. Alternatively, uninstalling the ColorX app removes all associated application files instantly.

Method 2: Contact Us to Delete Diagnostic Records

If you have contacted our support team previously or wish to request the deletion of any anonymous technical logs (such as crash diagnostics associated with your device IP):

  1. Send an email to latentcode1@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My ColorX Data.
  3. Mention your device model or details of your prior support ticket so we can locate and remove any records.

What Will Be Deleted

  • All saved artwork and colored drawings stored in local cache.
  • Saved color palettes, brush presets, and unlocked page progress.
  • Any support correspondence or support tickets tied to your email.

Data That May Be Retained

Non-identifiable, aggregated crash analytics (which contain zero personal details or user identifiers) may remain in standard third-party diagnostic tools (such as Firebase Crashlytics) until automated log retention cycles expire.

Processing Time

Clearing device storage removes your data instantly. Email deletion requests for support communications are processed within 7 business days.

Need Help?

If you are a parent or guardian with questions regarding your child's data or our privacy standards, contact us at: latentcode1@gmail.com.
