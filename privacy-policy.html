Privacy Policy for ColorX

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Welcome to ColorX ("we", "our", or "us"). We create fun, creative coloring experiences for children. Protecting the privacy of our young users and providing a safe digital environment is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage in compliance with child privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K).

Children’s Privacy & Parental Rights

ColorX is designed for families and children, including kids under the age of 13. We follow strict privacy standards:

  • No Personal Information: We do not knowingly collect, require, or solicit personal information (such as names, home addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses) from children.
  • No Account Required: Children can enjoy coloring pages without creating an account or registering personal details.
  • Local Storage: Saved drawings, coloring progress, and custom palette selections are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Parental Controls: Any external links, in-app purchases, or settings menus are protected behind an adult-gate/parental challenge to prevent accidental interaction by children.

Information We Collect

To ensure smooth app performance and resolve technical issues, we may collect minimal, non-personally identifiable technical data automatically, such as:

  • Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution
  • App performance metrics (such as crash logs and loading speeds)
  • Anonymous, aggregated gameplay interactions (e.g., favorite coloring tools or completed pages)

Third-Party Services

ColorX works only with trusted service providers who comply with child safety and privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services: Core application support and stability.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous crash reporting to diagnose technical bugs (configured to avoid collecting personal identifiers).
  • Kid-Safe Advertising (if applicable): If ads are present, they are served strictly through COPPA-compliant, child-directed ad networks that display contextual ads only (no behavioral tracking or profiling).

Data Security

We implement strict administrative and technical measures to safeguard device data and ensure that our application remains secure and safe for young users.

Parental Inquiries and Deletion Requests

Parents and legal guardians have the right to review, request deletion of, or refuse further collection of any technical data associated with their child's device at any time.

Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements to ColorX or updates in child privacy laws. Any updates will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date.
