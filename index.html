Privacy Policy for ColorX

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Welcome to ColorX ("we", "our", or "us"). ColorX is an interactive coloring and creativity application created for children and families. Protecting the privacy and safety of children online is our highest priority.

This Privacy Policy clearly outlines our data practices in strict compliance with:

  • Google Play Families Policy
  • Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) (US)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K) (EU/UK)

1. Age-Appropriate Content Guarantee

All content within ColorX—including line art templates, coloring tools, animations, audio, and visual themes—is thoroughly reviewed to be 100% child-friendly, constructive, and age-appropriate for children under 13.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

ColorX is built with a zero-friction, privacy-by-design architecture:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect, request, store, or transmit names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, biometric data, or precise geolocation.
  • No Account or Sign-In: Children do not need an account, profile, or password to use any part of the app.
  • No Behavioral Tracking or Profiling: We do not track children across third-party apps or websites. We do not collect or use the Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad profiling.

3. 100% Local Device Storage

All drawings, paint strokes, customized artworks, and saved masterpieces created by your child are stored strictly within the app’s private local sandbox storage on your device. No user drawings are uploaded to remote cloud servers.

4. Advertising Standards (Google Play-Certified Ad Networks Only)

If advertisements are displayed in ColorX, we strictly enforce all Google Play Families Advertising requirements:

  • Google Play-Certified Ad Networks Only: Any advertising SDKs integrated into ColorX are strictly sourced from the official Google Play-certified ad networks for children.
  • Child-Directed Treatment: All ad requests are explicitly flagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true (under COPPA) and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true (under GDPR).
  • Contextual Ads Only: Ads shown to children are strictly contextual and non-personalized. We prohibit interest-based, behavioral, or retargeted advertising.
  • No Deceptive Ads: We do not serve disruptive ads, ads that simulate app buttons, or inappropriate content.

5. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

ColorX requests only minimal permissions essential for creative painting activities:

  • Photo Library / Storage Access: Requested only when a user chooses to save an artwork to the device gallery or import a black-and-white coloring outline.
  • Camera (Optional): Used solely if the user chooses to take a picture of a physical coloring book page to colorize. Image processing occurs locally in real-time and is never sent online.

6. Parental Rights & Data Control

Parents and legal guardians retain full authority over their child's device data:

  • Because all drawings and settings are saved locally, parents can delete all saved artworks at any time from within the app’s Gallery or by clearing app storage in device Settings.
  • Parents have the right to contact us at any time to ask questions or verify our child privacy protections.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to maintain alignment with evolving Google Play Families policies or privacy laws. Any changes will be published here with an updated Effective Date.
